# Design the learning experience

Translate the requested audience and prerequisites into observable objectives. Connect each objective to an interaction and a question or task that demonstrates understanding. Explain scientific assumptions and the meaning of controls and outputs, including units.

Use existing modules as pedagogical examples only where they suit the audience. Pair changes to controls with prompts such as predict, explore, observe, and explain. Distinguish observed fit from evidence of generalization or physical validity.

Provide at least three questions with answers or feedback for a complete pilot lesson; scale assessment to the actual task for smaller edits. Include a likely misconception and an explanation addressing it. Record curriculum assumptions and criteria requiring subject-expert review; do not claim measured learning gains from content review.

Return learning content and criterion-to-verification mapping. Keep learner-facing material focused on the scientific task rather than agent implementation details.
